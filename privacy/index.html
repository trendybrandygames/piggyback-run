Privacy Policy for Piggyback Run

Effective date: November 24, 2026
Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Piggyback Run (the “Game”) is developed and published by Andrei Gavrilov pr., Serbia (“we”, “us”, or “our”).

This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, processed, stored, and shared when you use the Game.

We are committed to protecting the privacy of all players, including children. Piggyback Run is designed to minimize the collection and processing of personal information.

2. Information Collection and Use

Piggyback Run is designed as an offline game and does not require users to create an account, register, or provide personal information to play.

We do not operate servers that collect gameplay or player information.

The Game does not use:

  • Advertising or advertising tracking services.
  • Third-party analytics services.
  • Third-party crash reporting services.
  • Online leaderboards.
  • User accounts or online profiles.
  • Multiplayer, messaging, or chat services.

The Game's ordinary gameplay does not require the collection of names, email addresses, telephone numbers, location information, or advertising identifiers.

Limited information may be processed in connection with in-app purchases, operating system functionality, and voluntary support inquiries, as described below.

3. Locally Stored Information

The Game stores information locally on the user's device to provide its functionality and preserve the player's experience between gaming sessions.

This information may include:

  • Game progress.
  • High scores and local leaderboard entries.
  • Unlocked characters and game content.
  • Gameplay preferences and settings.
  • Information necessary to manage purchased game content.

We use this information solely to provide the Game's functionality.

The Game does not transmit gameplay data to developer-operated servers or provide its own cloud synchronization service.

Depending on the device's operating system, backup configuration, and user settings, Android may include locally stored application data in its backup or device migration services. Such services are governed by Google's applicable privacy policies and the user's device settings.

4. In-App Purchases

Piggyback Run offers in-app purchases through Google Play Billing.

Although ordinary gameplay is available offline, an internet connection and access to Google Play services may be required to complete, verify, or restore purchases.

Google processes payment transactions and may collect information necessary to provide payment services, including account information, payment information, device information, and transaction details.

We do not directly collect, process, or store users' credit card numbers, bank account details, or other payment credentials.

The Game may access purchase-related information provided by Google Play, including product identifiers, purchase tokens, transaction status, and purchase acknowledgments, to verify purchases, unlock purchased content, restore purchases, and prevent unauthorized access to paid content.

Google may also make transaction and financial records available to us through its developer services for purchase management, refunds, accounting, and legal compliance.

Google's processing of personal information is governed by its Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Parents and legal guardians are encouraged to use the parental controls and purchase approval options available through Google Play.

5. Advertising and Analytics

Piggyback Run does not display advertisements.

The Game does not integrate advertising networks, behavioral tracking systems, third-party analytics services, or third-party crash reporting services.

We do not use personal information for targeted advertising, behavioral profiling, or marketing to children.

6. Screenshot Sharing

The Game allows users to generate screenshots of gameplay and voluntarily share them using Android's native sharing functionality.

Screenshots are generated locally on the user's device and are not automatically uploaded to servers operated by us.

Shared content may include gameplay images, scores, and a public link to the Game or its website.

Users choose whether to share content and which external application or service to use for sharing.

Once content is shared with an external application or service, its processing is subject to that application's or service's own privacy policy.

We do not control the subsequent distribution, storage, or use of voluntarily shared content by third parties.

7. Children's Privacy

Piggyback Run is intended for a general audience that includes children.

The Game is designed to provide the same privacy protections to all players, regardless of age.

We do not require children to create accounts, provide their names, disclose their ages, or submit personal contact information to play the Game.

The Game does not contain personalized advertising, behavioral tracking, online chat, or public player profiles.

Google Play may process information in connection with application distribution, purchases, and operating system services as described in this Privacy Policy.

Parents and legal guardians are encouraged to supervise children's use of external sharing functionality and in-app purchases.

If we become aware that we have collected a child's personal information without authorization required by applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to delete that information.

Parents and legal guardians may contact us to request information about personal data relating to their child, request its deletion, or exercise other applicable privacy rights.

Privacy inquiries may be sent to: trendybrandy.games@gmail.com

The Android version of Piggyback Run uses Google Play services for application distribution and in-app purchases.

Android may also provide system-level functionality, including application backup, sharing, and device migration.

Additional information about Google's privacy practices is available at:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Terms of Service

GitHub's privacy practices are described at: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

9. Support and Communications

If you voluntarily contact us by email, we may process your email address, the contents of your message, and any information you choose to provide.

We use this information to:

  • Respond to questions and support requests.
  • Investigate technical problems.
  • Resolve purchase-related issues.
  • Respond to privacy requests.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use support communications to create advertising profiles or send unsolicited marketing communications.

Where applicable, we process this information based on our legitimate interests in providing support and maintaining the Game, fulfilling contractual obligations, or complying with legal requirements.

Children should ask a parent or legal guardian to contact us on their behalf when support is required.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored game information is retained on the user's device until it is removed by the user or the operating system.

Users can delete locally stored game information through Android's application storage settings or by uninstalling the Game, subject to any existing backup or restoration settings.

Copies of application data retained by Android backup services may be managed through the user's Google account and device settings.

Because Piggyback Run does not provide user accounts, there is no Game account that users need to delete.

Purchase records may be retained by Google and, where applicable, by us for transaction management, refunds, accounting, fraud prevention, and compliance with legal obligations.

Support communications are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the relevant inquiry, manage related disputes, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

Users and their parents or legal guardians may request deletion of personal information held by us by contacting trendybrandy.games@gmail.com.

We will respond to such requests within the timeframes required by applicable law. Certain information may be retained where necessary to comply with legal obligations or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Deleting locally stored game information does not necessarily delete purchase records maintained by Google or content previously shared through external services.

11. Data Security

We minimize privacy risks by designing Piggyback Run to operate offline and by avoiding unnecessary collection and transmission of personal information.

Locally stored application information is subject to the storage and security mechanisms provided by Android.

Payment processing is handled through Google Play Billing rather than through payment systems operated by us.

We take reasonable precautions to protect any personal information received through support communications and developer services against unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

However, no method of electronic storage or communication can guarantee absolute security.

12. International Data Processing

We operate from Serbia.

Third-party service providers, including Google, GitHub, and email service providers, may process information in other countries in accordance with their applicable privacy policies and legal obligations.

Where applicable data protection legislation requires safeguards for international transfers of personal information under our control, we use appropriate safeguards as required by law.
